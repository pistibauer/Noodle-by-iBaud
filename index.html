Logging shouldn't be a chore

Most calorie trackers fail for the same reason: typing every meal into a search box is tedious, and after two weeks you quietly stop. Noodle is built around one idea — logging should take seconds, and your day should make sense at a glance.

📷

On-device AI recognises your food and suggests calories. Nothing is uploaded.

🎙️

Just say it

“A bowl of ramen with an egg” is understood, matched and logged.

📊

Barcode & search

Exact nutrition for packaged food, plus 600,000+ foods from the USDA database.

🕘

From history

Most meals repeat. Re-log anything you've eaten before in two taps.

One number for your whole day

The On-Track ring folds calories eaten, calories burned and your target into a single score — so you know where you stand without doing mental arithmetic.

More than calories

Everything that actually moves the needle, in one app.

Real energy balance

Reads workouts and daily movement from Apple Health, then folds them into your target.

Honest goals

Set a goal weight and date — Noodle tells you if it's achievable at a healthy 1–2 lb per week, or suggests a realistic date.

Weight & hydration

Trend charts with goal lines, and a water target personalised to your body and activity.

Trends that make sense

Week, month, quarter or year — calories, protein, carbs, activity by type and hydration.

Privacy, properly

Your data stays on your iPhone. We don't have servers that hold your health information, because we never collect it.

  • No account to create, and no sign-in
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking
  • Food photos analysed on-device and never uploaded
  • Apple Health access is read-only and never used for advertising
