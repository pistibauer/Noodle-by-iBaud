Getting started

Noodle is a calorie and wellness tracker built around fast logging. There's no account to create — open the app and start logging.

  1. Open Targets and set your units, profile and daily calorie goal.
  2. Tap the + button to log food.
  3. Check the Today ring to see how your day is going.

Frequently asked questions

What are the four ways to log food?

  • Scan — point the camera at your plate; recognition runs on your device.
  • Speak — say what you ate, e.g. "a bowl of ramen with an egg".
  • Barcode — scan packaged food for exact nutrition.
  • From history — re-log anything you've eaten before in two taps.

What does the number in the ring mean?

That's your On-Track score — how close your intake is to your daily budget, which is your calorie target plus any calories you've burned through activity. It peaks at 100 when you land on your budget, and falls off if you eat well under or well over it. It naturally starts low each morning and climbs as you log meals.

The AI got my food wrong. What can I do?

Food recognition gives a suggestion with a confidence percentage, and it never logs anything by itself — you always confirm the portion first. If the guess is wrong, cancel and use Search or Barcode instead, which pull exact figures from the USDA nutrition database. Portion size in particular is an estimate, so adjust it to match what you actually ate.

How do I fix or delete an entry?

On the Today or History screen, tap an entry (or swipe it right) to change its quantity, meal, or date and time. Swipe left to delete it. Just after adding food, an Undo button also appears briefly at the top of the screen.

Can I log meals for a past day?

Yes. In the portion screen, open the When section and pick any past date and time. You can also change the date of an existing entry by editing it.

How do I switch between pounds and kilograms?

Go to Targets › Units and choose Imperial or International. Weights, portions and volumes update everywhere in the app. Your data isn't changed — only how it's displayed.

Why is my calorie burn missing or zero?

Activity comes from Apple Health. Check that you've allowed access in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices › Noodle. Noodle only reads activity data — it never writes to Apple Health.

The camera, microphone or notifications aren't working.

Open Settings on your iPhone, scroll to Noodle, and check that the relevant permission is enabled. Camera scanning and voice logging need a physical device with a camera and microphone.

How do I set up meal reminders?

Go to Targets › Meal reminders, turn them on, and pick a time for each meal. You'll only be reminded if that meal hasn't been logged yet.

How is my water target calculated?

It starts from established adequate-intake guidance for beverages (about 2.5 L a day for men and 2 L for women), then scales with your body weight and activity level. You can set your own target at any time from the slider icon on the hydration screen.

Where is my data stored? Can I get it back?

Everything is stored locally on your iPhone. There is no account and no cloud copy, which means your data is private — but it also means that deleting the app permanently erases your history. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I delete my data?

Delete individual entries by swiping them, or remove everything by deleting the app from your iPhone.

Requirements

  • iPhone running a recent version of iOS
  • Camera required for food scanning and barcodes
  • Microphone required for voice logging
  • Apple Health (optional) for activity and energy balance
  • An internet connection is needed only for food database lookups
