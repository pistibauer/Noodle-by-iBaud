Privacy Policy

Noodle by iBaud

Last updated: 1 September 2026

The short version. Noodle keeps your data on your iPhone. We do not have user accounts, we do not run analytics or advertising, we do not sell or share your information, and your health data never leaves your device except where you explicitly ask iOS to share it.

Who we are

Noodle ("the app") is developed by iBaud ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does with information on your device.

Information stored on your device

Everything you log — meals and portions, calorie and macronutrient totals, daily targets, weigh-ins, water intake, and your profile details (sex, age, height, weight, activity level, goals) — is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's on-device database. We do not operate servers that receive, store, or process this information.

Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you grant permission, Noodle reads activity information from Apple Health — active energy burned, workouts, and step count — to calculate your daily energy balance. This access is read-only: the app does not write to Apple Health.

  • Health data is used only to provide features inside the app.
  • Health data is never used for advertising or marketing.
  • Health data is never sold, shared, or disclosed to third parties, data brokers, advertising networks, or analytics services.
  • Health data is not transmitted off your device by the app.

You can review or revoke this access at any time in Settings › Health › Data Access & Devices, or in the Health app under Sharing.

Camera

With your permission, Noodle uses the camera to recognise food and to scan barcodes. Image analysis runs entirely on your device using Apple's on-device machine-learning frameworks. Photos taken for recognition are processed in memory and are not saved, stored, or uploaded anywhere by the app, and are not added to your photo library.

Microphone and speech recognition

With your permission, Noodle lets you log food by speaking. Transcription uses Apple's Speech framework. The app requests on-device recognition where your device supports it. On devices or in languages where on-device recognition is unavailable, iOS may send audio to Apple for processing under Apple's privacy policy. We do not receive, retain, or store your audio recordings.

Food database lookups

When you search for a food or scan a barcode, the app sends that search term or barcode number to the USDA FoodData Central public nutrition database in order to retrieve nutrition information. These requests contain only the search text or barcode. They do not include your name, account details, health data, location, or any identifier that we associate with you. Noodle works offline for everything except these lookups.

What we do not do

  • No user accounts and no sign-in.
  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, or tracking SDKs.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no data shared with data brokers.
  • No selling of personal information.
  • No location collection.

Children

Noodle is a general wellness app intended for adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your control over your data

Because your information is stored on your device, you control it directly. You can edit or delete individual entries inside the app, and deleting the app from your iPhone removes all of its stored data. Camera, microphone, speech, and Health permissions can be changed at any time in the iOS Settings app.

Not medical advice

Noodle is a general wellness and informational tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Calorie, nutrition, hydration, and weight-change figures — including those estimated by AI image recognition — are approximations and may be inaccurate. Always confirm estimates before relying on them, and consult a qualified healthcare professional before making decisions about your diet, hydration, exercise, or health.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and publish the revised version here.
